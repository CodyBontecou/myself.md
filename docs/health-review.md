Use `list_cloud_exports` to discover authorized health files for one phone/profile, including every file whose records cover the requested period. Upload dates alone are not record coverage. Call `review_activity_sleep` with `exportIds`, inclusive `start`/`end` dates (1–31 completed local days) and `timezone` (IANA).

The card displays daily step bars and sleep timing. In day detail, recorded stage durations use a shared zero-to-local-day-hours scale. Each label is unioned separately; conflicting labels may overlap and must not be summed. Structured `stageDurations` gives agents the same values. The current renderer resource is v2, with the v1 URI retained. Choose a source, then a day to inspect its sleep interval list. A compact overview retains the whole loaded period. These controls use returned state; they create no additional reads. Change dates conversationally through the same tool. Missing days are unknown. Even days with records may be incomplete; no clinical or causal interpretation is generated.

HealthKit `HKQuantityTypeIdentifierStepCount` accepts finite nonnegative `quantity` in `count`; Health Connect `Steps` accepts `count`. Separate source revision/data-origin and device identity prevents combining providers. Exact repeated native records are removed. Overlapping and midnight-crossing step samples are excluded rather than prorated. The graph uses a shared zero baseline within each selected source.

HealthKit `HKCategoryTypeIdentifierSleepAnalysis` maps numeric in-bed, asleep, awake, core, deep and REM categories. Health Connect `SleepSession` maps its explicit numeric stages, keeping unknown/awake/session-only intervals distinct. Local-day intervals are clipped at real timezone boundaries, including 23/25-hour days. Asleep/core/light/deep/REM intervals are unioned to avoid double counting. In-bed and awake intervals alone cannot establish asleep hours. Contradictory overlapping provider classifications remain visible in interval details; the summary is observed asleep-labeled duration, not an adjudicated sleep measurement. Calendar-day portions are not whole-night totals. WHOOP `whoop.sleep` records now use whole-session stage summaries; other metrics remain unsupported. WHOOP sessions are assigned to their local end day, including naps, and are never split or placed on a stage timeline. See the WHOOP support section below.

Each page uses the normal cloud read allowance and access history. Failed calls may still have consumed successful reads. Up to 32 exports and 200 pages are allowed; exceeding the page bound fails without returning a partial review. Partial export status and exclusions remain visible. The tool cannot capture phone data, grant itself access or change selections. Dataset labels are rendered as text.

## WHOOP sleep support — 22 October 2026

`whoop.sleep` records with source `whoop` expose whole-session start/end, nap status, score state, stage durations and export provenance through the same authorized review tool. WHOOP supplies aggregate millisecond stage summaries rather than stage timestamps ([provider data model](https://developer.whoop.com/docs/developing/user-data/sleep/)). Asleep duration sums Light, slow-wave/Deep and REM, excluding Awake and No data. Session totals belong to the local date of session end in the requested IANA timezone, including exact midnight. They are not calendar-day portions and are not prorated. The card uses duration bars and session details for WHOOP; HealthKit/Health Connect retain their interval timelines.

Scored numeric summaries must contain finite, nonnegative integer milliseconds with combined stage durations no greater than in-bed duration (one-millisecond tolerance). Pending, unscorable or malformed summaries retain session details with unknown duration. Overlapping sessions make the affected daily totals unknown, including overlaps across end dates. WHOOP accounts remain separate from OS/device series. Repeated provider session IDs are counted once; the newest comparable `updated_at` revision wins. Conflicting equal/unknown revisions are excluded rather than chosen arbitrarily. No clinical targets, inferred stage order, data capture or access changes are added. The new v3 resource retains v1/v2 read aliases.
