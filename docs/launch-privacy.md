## Retention boundaries

Live query access expires five minutes from request creation; completion does not extend it. The service removes the whole result on invalidating revocation, forgetting, cleanup after expiry or process end. Logical expiry is distinct from immediate memory erasure: an idle process can retain an inaccessible expired result until its next cleanup. Persisted Worker job metadata omits record bodies. Separate request history retains metadata under its existing 90-day cleanup-on-access rule.

Deletion status remains usable for seven days. Its directory routing row is removed by the next daily cleanup, normally within one further day. Completed accounts retain a pseudonymous rejection tombstone. Purchase identifiers retain neither the account link nor purchase proof. Analytics events and aliases are erased; the disabled pseudonymous account preference has no automatic expiry, and anonymous retry-blocking hashes remain for 180 days. These guards prevent delayed requests from recreating erased state; they do not contain analytics events.

Cloud, browser and agent deletion cannot recall files already sent to another phone, an external destination or an AI provider. Phone-initiated cleanup additionally removes its own account-owned local data after remote acceptance. OS health/screen-time stores, store subscriptions and refunds remain separate owner/platform actions.
