# Heart-rate overlays in ChatGPT

Discover authorized cloud exports first, then call `review_heart_rate_context` with `healthExportIds`, `contextExportIds`, `context` (`location` or `screen_time`), inclusive `start`/`end`, and `timezone`. Use one profile per list on the same owned phone. File upload dates do not establish record coverage. The two lists can reference the same mixed-domain file; each domain read uses the normal metered path.

Location uses the nearest recorded heart-rate timestamp within `maxOffsetSeconds` (default 300, range 0–900). Ties prefer earlier samples. The card colors recorded coordinates by BPM and exposes GPS accuracy, signed time offsets, sample timestamps/interval ends and export provenance. Missing matches remain gray. Coordinates use degrees rather than a distance-preserving map; no routes, visits or dwell times are inferred.

Screen time shows colored hourly bars directly in the all-days overview. Selecting an hour opens its day and matched samples. Bars keep a common period-wide duration scale. Screen time uses validated observed app usage confined to local clock hours. Bar lengths show minutes; colors show the unweighted mean of heart-rate samples within that hour. Detail gives sample count/range, app totals and exact observations. A shared time axis shows context intervals and individual heart-rate dots. Hourly usage does not locate the exact moments of app use, so the association cannot establish heart rate while actively using a particular app. Daily and hour-crossing aggregates remain unallocated. Repeated DST hours use the existing combined clock-hour model and retain actual instant boundaries.

HealthKit HeartRate quantities and quantitySeries plus Health Connect HeartRate samples are accepted in `count/min` or `bpm`. Other health metrics are excluded and counted. Source/device identities remain separate; exact repeats are deduplicated with export IDs retained. Quantity intervals may summarize more than an instant; the timestamp anchor and end remain visible. The observed color range is not a clinical classification. No interpolation or causal conclusions are generated.

Day/source/detail/sample navigation uses the returned state without further reads. Coordinate and timeline plots show at most 1,000 points/samples, including the selected observation; details paginate 25 context observations and show up to 100 matched samples for an hour, with the complete sample list retained in the structured result. The bounded request accepts 32 files per domain list and 200 total pages, at most 10,000 accepted heart samples and eight heart sources. Ownership, selections, current authorization and file identity are checked across both domain reads. Failures do not return partial overlays but can consume completed reads. Partial files and exclusion counts stay visible.
