## Living globe

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished square app icon concept for myself.md, a private personal-data app bringing together location, time and health. A single standalone full-bleed 1024x1024 opaque square asset, no presentation board, no caption, no words, no device mockup. Refined restrained native app aesthetic, strong recognizable silhouette at 48px, generous margin around a centered symbol, crisp edges. Background warm off-white (#F7F7F4), charcoal primary geometry with restrained cobalt blue accent. No outer rounded-square mask; OS handles corners. Avoid tiny detail, gradients, decorative sparkles, generic AI branding. Direction 1 — Living globe. One bold circular globe symbol, two curved meridians and one equator maximum. Integrate a single continuous cobalt route/pulse into the equator: a subtle smooth rise and fall suggesting a heartbeat, ending in one small location dot. Globe perimeter has one deliberate gap with a tiny clock-like endpoint so location, elapsed time and life read as one unified mark. Flat graphic, precise and elegant, no continents.
